MODES
Play outside the story: pick a way to play.
ARCADE
Practice any trial on its own, outside the story. No quest progress here, just the mechanic.
ENDLESS
Survive wave after wave of foes drawn from every corner of the journey. Bigger matches bank more charge on the strike window, up to a cap. Every 5th wave is one of the great bosses.
BOSS RUSH
Face all five great bosses in random order, then the guardian of Mother's Crown itself, three phases deep. Full gear, full challenge. HP, Mana, and Charge reset fresh before every single fight. Back out mid-run and you lose all progress, so commit before you begin.
OPTIONS
Speeds through the enemy's turn instead of playing it out. Damage still applies, it just resolves almost instantly.
